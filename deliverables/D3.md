# System Description

Many music fans fall into repetitive listening habits because they are unaware of their own patterns and depend on algorithmic playlists, which costs them intentionality and discovery. For heavy music listeners who care about how their music is delivered, or who want to share their listening habits, Interlude is a music tracker that integrates directly into their streaming service of choice, unlike Last.fm. Its value proposition is an automatic music diary that tracks and visualizes listening history over time, while using that history and the time of day to recommend new music and resurface older songs.

Each **User** has a *name*, *email*, *spotify_id*, and *applemusic_id*. A **User** ***follows*** other **Users** to view their stats. Whenever a **User** ***listens*** to a track, the system records a **SongListen** with a *timestamp*. Each **Song** ***tracks*** its **SongListens** and has an *id*, *title*, *album*, and *artist*. A **Song** is either a **SpotifySong** or an **AppleMusicSong**.

To retrieve metadata, each **Song** ***sends to*** a **ServiceIdToITunesTrack** with a *service_id*, and an **ITunesTrack** ***maps_to*** it. An **ITunesTrack** has an *id*, *name*, *censored_name*, *view_url*, *preview_url*, *artwork_url_60*, *artwork_url_100*, *price*, *explicitness*, *disc_count*, *disc_number*, *track_count*, *track_number*, *track_time_millis*, *is_streamable*, *country*, *currency*, *release_date*, and *primary_genre_name*. An **ITunesCollection** ***includes*** tracks and has an *id*, *name*, *censored_name*, *view_url*, *artwork_url_60*, *artwork_url_100*, *price*, *explicitness*, *content_advisory_rating*, *disc_count*, *disc_number*, *track_count*, *copyright*, *country*, *currency*, *release_date*, and *primary_genre_name*. An **ITunesArtist** ***creates*** tracks and collections and has an *id*, *name*, *link_url*, *amg_artist_id*, *primary_genre_name*, and *primary_genre_id*. An **ITunesTrackSampleMood** ***samples*** each track with a *mood*, *tempo*, *brightness*, *energy*, *key*, *acousticness*, *instrumentalness*, *liveness*, *danceability*, *arousal*, *valence*, and *speechiness*.

A **Recommendation** ***recommends*** music to a **User** and ***chooses*** a **Song**, recording the *time_of_day*, the *reason*, and whether it was resurfaced through *is_resurfaced*. A **User** also ***creates*** **JournalEntries** with an *entry_date* and *text* as an optional manual supplement.
