# Interviews summary

## Interview 1
### Who was interviewed
Seth Bowling - a 19 y/o who uses Spotify on the daily, uses both desktop and mobile clients.
### Who participated in the interview
Daniel Raygoza
### Interview date
9/12/26
### Customer segement represented in the interview
Daily music listener who cares about their Spotify wrapped and sorting and filtering their music by genres.
### Key insights from the interview
- Would want to search, sort, and filter playlists by genre and mood. Also shuffling with biases towards those properties.
- Desired improved discover weekly to better match their preferences.
- Would want the benefits of the data that is collected, but wouldn't be journaling their mood.
### Representative quotes
- "This could be a mental thing, but I feel like whenever I hit shuffle on like my big playlist, it's always like the same songs."
- "My Discover weekly hasn't been good in year, to be honest... like I'll listen to a song for like a couple of days, and then my Discover weekly will be like duplicates of that song. Um, and it seems like if I don't have one song that I listen to, it's very like random."
- "I use Discover weekly... which ends up being songs I don't really listen to again... I just spend like a few minutes every session like skipping through songs. Sometimes when I shuffle based on genre, it put music that aren't really in that genre, or really, belong in a subgenre, which I don't want to listen to right now."
- "(About Spotify wrapped) I think that it's cool but I don't really do like anything with that information."
- "(About music habit tracking) That's something I'd use, but maybe less for the tracking music habits."
### Assessment
#### Must have
- Better Spotify discover weekly
- Better playlist interactions based off genre and mood
#### Nice to have
- A place to view music statistics
#### Don't care
- Journaling listening moods

## Interview 2
### Who was interviewed
Isaiah Ray - a 19 y/o who formerly used Apple Music then migrated to Illusi.
Very into all types of music and listens to music the majority of everyday.
### Who participated in the interview
Daniel Raygoza
### Interview date
9/13/26
### Customer segement represented in the interview
Daily music listener who cares about discovering good music in their taste.
### Key insights from the interview
- No need to look at own statistics, more value came from what could actually be done within the music apps with the data that we collect.
- Saw improvements to shuffling music and discovering new music with stations.
### Representative quotes
- "(About Apple Music Replay) I don't give a single care, because if it's not on Spotify, like, people only really care about Spotify wrapped anyways... I don't do anything useful with that information."
- "Like a music diary? That'd be like Instagram notes the app. But, yeah I'd use something like that, though not sure how often.
    If I could just let it track my music automatically and give me better recommendations then I'd fosholi use it."
- "Maybe slightly better music stations, and being able to shuffle songs in a way that I don't have to constantly skip them, you know like..."
### Assessment
#### Must have
- A mobile version of any extra music related features.
- Custom music shuffling.
#### Nice to have
- Better stations
- Music Diary
#### Don't care
- Wrapped/Replay
- Viewing own statistics.

## Interview 5

### Who was interviewed
Alex Porras
### Who participated in the interview
Ronald Milton
### Interview date
9/17/2026
### Customer segement represented in the interview
Heavy music listener/Spotify user with opinions on Spotify Wrapped
### Key insights from the interview
- Would love to see statistics about their least favorite songs/genres, not just most favorite
- Seemed to prefer if favorite songs were based off plays rather than time listened
- Spotify has poor shuffling features/recommendations 
- Spotify Wrapped is heavily shared on social media, so social features would used rather heavily
### Representative quotes
- "Whether you listen to a 3 minute song or a 1 minute song it should be reflected by how many times you chose to listen to that song"
- "Spotify Wrapped always focuses on your most favorite, I think it should also go into your least favorite, I think that would make it a lot more enjoyable"
### Assessment
#### Must Have
- Least Favorite Songs
- Most Skipped Songs
#### Nice to have
- Favorite Songs by Plays
- Better Recommendations/Shuffling
- Social/Journaling Features
