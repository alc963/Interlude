# Introduction

Dispite having access to millions and millions of songs, many music fans find themselves losing the early magic and getting stuck in repetative listening patterns, missing the greatness of musical discovery. This gap between an avid listener's hard-stuck habits and the greater world is what Interlude is trying to solve. Using our web application, users can track and visualize their listening habits to break out of their musical echo chambers.

# Initial value proposition and customer segments

## Value Proposition

Insights about your music listening habits

## Customer Segments

- Heavy music listeners
- People who journal

# Interviews summary

## Interview 1 - Seth Bowling

### Who was interviewed

Seth Bowling - a 19 y/o who uses Spotify on the daily, uses both desktop and mobile clients.

### Who participated in the interview

Daniel Raygoza

### Interview date

9/12/26

### Customer segement represented in the interview

Daily music listener who cares about their Spotify wrapped and sorting and filtering their music by genres.

### Key insights from the interview

Would want to search, sort, and filter playlists by genre and mood. Also shuffling with biases towards those properties.

Desired improved discover weekly to better match their preferences.

Would want the benefits of the data that is collected, but wouldn't be journaling their mood.

### Representative quotes
Q: "What would your complaints with Spotify be?"
A: "This could be a mental thing, but I feel like whenever I hit shuffle on like my big playlist, it's always like the same songs."
\
\
Q: "How are Spotify's recommendations?"
A: "My Discover weekly hasn't been good in year, to be honest... like I'll listen to a song for like a couple of days, and then my Discover weekly will be like duplicates of that song. Um, and it seems like if I don't have one song that I listen to, it's very like random."
\
\
Q: "Do you most the time just listen to your own playlists or the recomendations or what do you usually try to do?..."
A: "I use Discover weekly... which ends up being songs I don't really listen to again... I just spend like a few minutes every session like skipping through songs. Sometimes when I shuffle based on genre, it put music that aren't really in that genre, or really, belong in a subgenre, which I don't want to listen to right now."
\
\
Q: "Do you care about Spotify wrapped and does it make you look at your music differently?"
A: "I think that it's cool but I don't really do like anything with that information."
\
\
Q: "If there was a service that could solve the problems you've listen while also giving you insights towards your music habits, would you use it?"
A: "That's something I'd use, but maybe less for the tracking music habits."

### Assessment

#### Must have

- Better Spotify discover weekly
- Better playlist interactions based off genre and mood

#### Nice to have

- A place to view music statistics

#### Don't care

- Journaling listening moods

## Interview 2 - Isaiah Ray

### Who was interviewed

Isaiah Ray - a 19 y/o who formerly used Apple Music then migrated to Illusi.
Very into all types of music and listens to music the majority of everyday.

### Who participated in the interview

Daniel Raygoza

### Interview date

9/13/26

### Customer segement represented in the interview

Daily music listener who cares about discovering good music in their taste.

### Key insights from the interview

No need to look at own statistics, more value came from what could actually be done within the music apps with the data that we collect.
Saw improvements to shuffling music and discovering new music with stations.

### Representative quotes

Q: "Do you care about Apple Music Replay?"
A: "I don't give a single care, because if it's not on Spotify, like, people only really care about Spotify wrapped anyways."

Q: "So would you say it's more of a 'cool little feature', but you don't really spend that much time on it..."
A: "Pretty much, I don't do anything useful with that information."

Q: "For better [music] recommendations tell me, would you ever write down what your thinking while listening to music?"
A: "Like a music diary? That'd be like Instagram notes the app. But, yeah I'd use something like that, though not sure how often.
    If I could just let it track my music automatically and give me better recommendations then I'd fosholi use it."

Q: "If you had a service that tracked your music listening habits, what value do you think it could provide?"
A: "Maybe slightly better music stations, and being able to shuffle songs in a way that I don't have to constantly skip them, you know like..."

### Assessment

#### Must have

- A mobile version of any extra music related features.
- Custom music shuffling.

#### Nice to have

- Better stations
- Music Diary

#### Don't care

- Wrapped/Replay
- Viewing own statistics.
